Beauty Makeup Guide

Foundation

Foundation helps to create a smooth base for makeup and evens out skin tone.

Different skin types require different foundations such as liquid, powder, or cream.

Eye Makeup

Eye makeup includes products like eyeshadow, eyeliner, and mascara to enhance the eyes.

Using different colors of eyeshadow can create bold or natural looks.

Lip Makeup

Lipstick adds color and style to the lips and completes the makeup look.

Lip gloss can also be applied to give lips a shiny and attractive finish.
